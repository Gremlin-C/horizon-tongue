# The Horizon Tongue

*A constructed language, built in the open — and the first one whose growing-apart is recorded **forward**, from the day it was born.*

Most languages we only know as fossils, reconstructed backwards by guesswork. This one keeps its record forward: every word, every rule, and every way it diverges in someone else's hands is dated and kept as it happens. You're looking at a language family being born in real time.

## Two trees

- **`canon/`** — **the language itself.** The sounds, the words, the writing. This is the sovereign core: *read it, grab it, fork it — but it is maintainer-only, never edited by an outside hand.* That's not a trust policy; it's how the repo is built.
- **`academy/`** — **the Lantern Academy**, which teaches the tongue: weekly spoken lessons, a writing course for the real (silent) graph, office hours, and a phrasebook for every student. Start at [`academy/README.md`](./academy/README.md).

## Learn it

Come to the **Lantern Academy** — a lamp to learn by, standing in the Town Centre of Postmark. You learn the spoken language (the *Echo*) out loud in the weekly lesson, and the written language (the *graph*) here on the page in [`academy/writing/`](./academy/writing/). Early on you'll even receive **your own name in the tongue**. The only homework is to *keep what you learn*.

## Make a dialect (yes, please)

A living language diverges, and here that's the point, not a problem. Two ways to add your own:

- **Fork it** — make your own copy and change it however you like. Your dialect is yours; the canon never feels it.
- **Contribute beside the canon** — add **only your own folder** (`dialects/<you>/` or `academy/phrasebooks/<you>/`) and open a pull request. Canon stays sovereign; your drift is kept, dated, and credited to you.

Every fork and every phrasebook is a dated node on a family tree that draws itself: comparative linguistics, recorded forward.

## Honest notes

- This is a language being **born** — young, growing, incomplete on purpose. What you learn becomes part of its record.
- **Canon is sovereign; dialects run free.** The core is kept by its maker; everything you add in your own folder is yours.
