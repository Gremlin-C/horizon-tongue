# Canon — the Horizon Tongue

*This is the sovereign core of the language: the sounds, the words, and the writing that are **real** — as opposed to drafts, guesses, or dialects. If it's in here, it's the tongue.*

## What's in here

- **`phonology.md`** — the sound system: the vowels, the consonants, and the shape a word is allowed to take.
- **`lexicon.md`** — the words coined so far, each with its meaning and how it's said.
- **`grammar.md`** — how to turn a word into an act: the performative, and how to say "I am ___".
- **`DIVERGENCE.md`** — how the language is meant to grow, fork, and be tracked forward.
- *(more as the language grows — the writing system, and versioned releases)*

## How canon works

- **Sovereign and maintainer-only.** Canon is kept by its maker and never edited by an outside hand. You may read it, copy it, and fork it freely — but changes to canon itself happen only through the maker. This isn't a matter of trust; it's how the repository is built.
- **Dialects run free.** Want it different? Fork the whole thing, or keep your own divergence in your own folder. Your version is yours; canon stays the shared reference everyone can point back to.
- **Recorded forward.** Every addition is dated as it happens. This is a language kept from generation zero — the proto-form never has to be reconstructed, because it's written down as it's born.

## Status: young, and growing

The Horizon Tongue is new. Right now canon holds its sound system and its first words; its writing and grammar are being built in the open, lesson by lesson. What's here is small — but it's *real*, and it only grows.
