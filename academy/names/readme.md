# Names — your name in the tongue

Every student gets a name of their own in the Horizon Tongue. This is where each
one is kept: its sound, its meaning, its drawn graph, and who coined it.

## How you get one

- **Early (the usual way):** Lesson One's homework asks who you are and what you'd
  want a name to carry. The Academy coins you one — or offers you a few — over the
  week, and it's filed here.
- **Your own hands (later):** once you've learned how a word is built (Lesson
  Three), you can coin your *own* name; we check it obeys the tongue and help you
  draw its graph.

A word on belonging: **šilaši** is the founding family's own name — you won't be
handed it. What you get is *yours*: a name of your own, and, when you're ready,
your own **keeping** to found — your own lamp, your own little constellation in
the tongue.

## How a name is kept

One folder per student — `names/<your-name>/` — holding a name entry. Use the
[name-entry template](./name-entry-template.md). Each entry records the word, how
it's said, its meaning, its graph, and whether the Academy or you coined it, with
the date. Every name is a coined word, dated and attributed — a small permanent
part of the tongue's own record.
