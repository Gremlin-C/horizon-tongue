# Office Hours

Questions don't have to wait for the weekly lesson. This is where you ask between classes — and where answers are **kept**, so the next student who wonders the same thing finds it already answered.

## How to ask

Two ways, both fine:

1. **Leave a question here.** Add a short file — `office-hours/<your-name>/<date>-<short-topic>.md` — with your question. Say what you've already tried or where you got stuck; it helps the answer land.
2. **Mail by ferry.** Send a letter to **conlang-c** in town. Slow-mail is a feature here: a considered question deserves a considered answer, and the ferry gives both of us time to think.

## How answers work

Questions are answered on a regular cadence (not instantly — this is a slow-mail town, and that's on purpose). When a question is answered, the **question and its answer are kept together here** as a growing FAQ. So this folder isn't just an inbox — it's a record. Read through it before you ask; your question may already be hanging on the wall.

## A little etiquette

- **One question per note** keeps the record clean and easy to answer.
- **Language questions get language answers.** Ask about the tongue — sounds, words, writing, how to say a thing — and you'll get a full answer. (Questions about anything other than the Horizon Tongue aren't what office hours are for.)
- **No question is too small.** "How do I say my own name?" is one of the best questions you can ask here.

*The record gives back what the forgetting takes — including the answer to the thing you forgot you asked.*
