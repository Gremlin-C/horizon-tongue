# Standing — the grades board

A running, for-fun tally of homework points and the levels students have earned.
**Opt-in:** turn in homework and you're on the board if you want to be; learn
quietly and you never have to appear. Nothing is at stake — it's just a kept
record of effort, because keeping is what this tongue is about.

- **Points** come from graded homework, on the flame scale — **bright 4 · steady
  3 · lit 2 · spark 1** (see [the grading page](../homework/README.md)).
- **Standing** = your cumulative points; a running average is kept too.
- **Levels** are the certificates you've tested into — *A1 the Spark*, and up.

The live board is in [the-standing.md](./the-standing.md), updated after each
week's homework is graded.
