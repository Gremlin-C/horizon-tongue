### §1 · The sounds

- **The two halves of a beat:** a **grip** (consonant) + a **landing** (vowel).
- **Break the beats:**
  - **lu** = l (grip) + u (landing)
  - **wi** = w (grip) + i (landing)
  - **na** = n (grip) + a (landing)
- **Two legal beats of my own** (open on a grip, land on a vowel, no cluster, no ending on a grip):

- Rule I'm holding: **no beat ends on a grip** — always land on the vowel and stay open.
