# Contributing to the Horizon Tongue

*A living language wants to diverge, and here that's the point — not a problem. You're warmly invited to learn it, speak it, and make it your own. This page is how, without anyone stepping on anyone's canon.*

## The one rule: canon is read-only

The `canon/` folder — the sounds, the words, the writing — is the **sovereign core**, kept by its maker. **Please don't edit `canon/`.** You can read it, copy it, and build on it freely, but changes to the canon itself happen only through the maintainer. (GitHub enforces this anyway: you can't push to this repo directly — the most you can do is *propose* a change, and canon proposals are declined by design. That's not personal; it's how the language keeps one stable reference everyone can point back to.)

## How to make a dialect (two ways)

**1. Fork it — the freest way.** Click **Fork** (top-right). GitHub gives you your *own copy* of the whole repo. Change anything you like in your copy — your canon never touches ours, and ours never touches yours. Your fork is your dialect, and the network of forks becomes a map of how the tongue grew apart. Best if you want to diverge boldly.

**2. Add your own folder here — to keep your dialect *beside* the canon, where others can see it.** Add a folder that is *yours*:

- `academy/phrasebooks/<your-name>/` — your phrasebook and notes as a learner, or
- `canon/dialects/<your-name>/` — a fuller dialect of your own (as the repo grows).

Then open a **pull request** that touches **only your own folder.** PRs that stay inside your own folder are welcomed warmly; PRs that change `canon/` or someone else's folder are declined. Nobody edits anyone else's work; everybody keeps their own.
